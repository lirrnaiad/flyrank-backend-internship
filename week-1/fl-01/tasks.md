# Recurring Tasks Breakdown

| Done | Reliance on AI | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Creating mining flashcards in Anki from Japanese light novel reading | Just me | Vocabulary I do not recognize the reading/definition are things that only I know, not AI. | 
| pwn.college challenges | Collaborate with AI | Although I usually do the challenges myself due to the bite-sized nature of pwn.college's challenges, I often discuss with the built-in AI to learn how one challenge could be solved more efficiently. I usually end up learning more that way than if I just know my own solution. |
| Writing capstone project manuscripts (Chapters 1–3) | Collaborate with AI | Capstone manuscripts are tough, and our professors usually have an AI detector, so letting it generate text and copy pasting it is a big no no. I write the content myself and have AI revew it. |
| AtCoder Beginner Contest | Just me | Competitive Programming competitions strictly forbid the use of AI! I don't even review with AI AFTER the competition despite it being allowed by that point, I usually write a writeup then compare mine with a top player's solution or their writeup itself. |
| AtCoder Scraper Project (frequent-vocab Anki deck builder) | Collaborate with AI | I'm just in the starting phase actually, but drafting the architecture on this had me really understand my lack of knowledge primarily because of concepts I didn't even consider, such as using a NLP library like SudachiPy paired with a Japanese dictionary to actually separate the words from each other (Japanese doesn't have spaces). AI helped me with knowing what these are in the first place, and it's my job to do the research myself. |
| Studying Python (for data, personal) | Collaborate with AI | Same rationale as in pwn.college. I use DataCamp and the bite-sized nature of it allows me to usually think for myself, but when there's a difficult problem I need help with, or a tricky problem I solved that I'd love to discuss and learn more from, I welcome AI with open arms. |
| Studying Java (for hireability in Japanese job market) | Collaborate with AI | Same as above, I'm using Chad Darby's Spring and Spring Boot course in Udemy |
| Advanced uni studying (e.g., Operating Systems) | Just me | There's a syllabus and recommended readings. Actually reading them and creating Anki flashcards out of it is more than enough for the tasks given by my uni 7 times out of 10. |
| Uni coding assignments/projects | Collaborate with AI | You learn the most when you do the code yourself! If I'm having trouble, I use AI and ask not for the code itself but to ask me questions to steer my thinking to lead me to the solution. |
| Uni paperwork assignments/projects | Delegate to AI with review | Most of the time the paperwork stuff is usually from minor subjects, and while they are arguably important for your overall character, I can't be half-assed to write it all myself knowing I could spend more time studying something related to my industry. I usually just write a skeleton and my thought process and let AI do most of the work. |
