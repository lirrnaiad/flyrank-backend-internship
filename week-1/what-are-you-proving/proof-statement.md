# Proof Statement

I'm a 4th-year BSIT student graduating mid-2027, targeting backend infrastructure roles at Japan-HQ firms based in NCR — recruiters who need real architectural judgment, not generic bilingual support. As backend lead on UEP-Smart Agriculture, I'm migrating our device communication from REST to MQTT: moving to a leaner serialization format to cut payload size, and choosing QoS levels that trade delivery guarantees against overhead for unreliable field networks. Because I'm the one initiating outbound — applications, FlyRank cohort networking, direct recruiter emails — my portfolio and resume have to do their work inside a thread I already opened: proving enough judgment, fast, that the resume becomes the artifact a recruiter forwards internally to a hiring manager. My JLPT N2 certification backs the language half of that judgment; my TELUS role proves it's applied, not just tested. Together they're the case for a fit as I move into final year.

## Why This Needs to Exist

As the brief says, only choose ONE claim, ONE person, and ONE action. Having a clear goal on what your portfolio will do will make it easier to actually map out your site, and to avoid scope creep. All in all, this proof statement will serve as a manifesto and will be the foundation of this project from planning until deployment.
